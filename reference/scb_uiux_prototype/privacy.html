Privacy & safeguarding

A clear public statement about respectful data and image use.

This prototype includes a dedicated policy layout for privacy, child safeguarding, cookies and website terms.

Data minimisation

Public forms should collect only what the school needs to respond. Sensitive records must use secure, purpose-built processes rather than general contact forms.

Children’s photographs

The school should record consent, avoid unnecessary identifying details, remove image metadata where appropriate and provide a clear removal-request process.

Administrator access

Every administrator should use an individual account, least-privilege permissions, secure authentication and auditable activity.
