# Content and Safeguarding Launch Checklist

Complete this checklist with the head teacher or delegated safeguarding owner before public launch.

## School Identity and Public Facts

- [ ] Confirm the official school name, motto, address, telephone, email and office hours in **Site settings**.
- [ ] Confirm that no placeholder fees, results, accreditation, contacts or statistics are published.
- [ ] Confirm that the exact protected school crest is unchanged.
- [ ] Review every navigation and footer link on mobile and desktop.

## Photographs and Galleries

- [ ] Review every public media record for clear alternative text and an appropriate caption.
- [ ] Confirm the consent reference for every identifiable child photograph.
- [ ] Confirm that restricted, private or unconfirmed-consent media does not appear publicly.
- [ ] Review crop and focal placement at 390 px, 768 px and 1440 px widths.
- [ ] Test album/category filters, keyboard opening, Escape closing and focus return in the gallery lightbox.
- [ ] Confirm that original metadata is absent from public derivatives.

## News, Events, FAQs and Downloads

- [ ] Verify names, titles, dates and venues in all launch news and events.
- [ ] Confirm the 15 August 2026 graduation story and its approved photographs.
- [ ] Verify every FAQ answer with an authorised user; edited answers must return to draft.
- [ ] Open every published download and confirm its version, date, MIME type and intended audience.
- [ ] Confirm that draft, review, archived and future content is absent from public search and sitemap output.

## Enquiries and Privacy

- [ ] Send one admissions enquiry and one contact message from a non-staff device.
- [ ] Confirm queue delivery, receipt, assignment, internal note privacy and tracked email reply history.
- [ ] Confirm approved data-retention periods and safe export handling.
- [ ] Confirm privacy, cookies and safeguarding text with the responsible school owner.

## Accounts and Operations

- [ ] Assign least-privilege permissions using `docs/permissions-matrix.md` and test every production role separately.
- [ ] Remove or disable all temporary accounts and replace temporary passwords.
- [ ] Confirm HTTPS, secure cookies, production debug-off state and authentication throttling.
- [ ] Run the queue worker, scheduler, health check, off-server backup and isolated restore drill.
- [ ] Record final responsive screenshots using `docs/design-parity-report.md`.
