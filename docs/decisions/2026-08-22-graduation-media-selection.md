## Processing and safeguarding

- Original JPEGs were decoded and re-encoded as WebP masters with a maximum 2,560-pixel edge and quality 82.
- Responsive 480, 960 and 1,600-pixel WebP variants were generated at quality 78.
- Re-encoding removed the original Canon/Lightroom EXIF metadata; the committed outputs contain no camera/EXIF markers.
- Masters and variants are served from private Laravel storage only after MySQL visibility, restriction and consent checks pass.
- Child photographs are marked consent-required and confirmed using the owner-supplied media authority reference. The hall photograph does not require child-media consent.
- The exact school logo was not modified.
